How to get a MakerWorld token
  1. Open MakerWorld and sign in to your Bambu account.
  2. Right-click the page and choose Inspect. In Chrome or Edge, open Application → Cookies; in Firefox, open Storage → Cookies; in Safari, use Develop → Show Web Inspector → Storage → Cookies (enable developer features in Safari’s Advanced settings if needed).
  3. Select makerworld.com, find the cookie named token, and copy its complete value.
  4. Paste it below, then choose Verify and save token.

You can complete sign-in in your browser and bring the token here.

Your token is stored on this BambuBoard instance and sent only to Bambu Lab. Signing out returns cloud widgets to their placeholders.
